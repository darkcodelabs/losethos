User skills required:
  • Knowledge of the C programming language.

Supported hardware:
  • 64-bit PC compatible computer such as a Corei7, Core2Duo or PentiumD
  • Most keyboards and mice.
  • Minimum 512 Meg RAM; Maximum 128 Gig RAM
  • HD Audio or internal PC Speakers
  • CD/DVD-ROMs (not SCSI).  Multi-session burns are not supported.
  • ATA and SATA hard drives

Unsupported hardware:
  • No modern graphics card modes besides VGA 640x480 16 color
  • No sound cards besides HD Audio
  • No networking
  • No USB devices (except keyboard and mouse in legacy mode.)
  • No printers
