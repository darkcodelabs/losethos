LoseThos SSE/MMX

Intel's SIMD (single instruction multiple data) capabilities apply to 128 bits 
of data.  You can do 16x8bit, 8x16bit, 4x32bit or 2x64bit operations.  LoseThos 
mostly uses 64-bit values.  In my opinion, two operations at once is not enough 
to make it worthwhile.  LoseThos is not for multi-media.  That's the primary 
usage of SSE/MMX, right?  I don't see anywhere where SSE/MMX would be useful in 
LoseThos.
