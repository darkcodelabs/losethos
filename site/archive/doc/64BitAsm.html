64-Bit Assembly Language

Here are counter-intuitive facts:

1)      XOR     EAX,EAX

Clears high 32-bits as well.

2)      MOV     RAX,0x0123456789ABCDEF
        MOV     EAX,0x123
The high 32 bits will be zero.

3)      PUSH    EAX
Pushes all 64-bits of RAX

4)      MOV     RAX,[0x1234]
This is RIP relative and only works within +/- 2Gig of the instruction.

5)      CALL    0x123456
This is RIP relative and only works within +/- 2Gig of the instruction.

6) To set the FS or GS registers:
        MOV     RDX,RAX
        SHR     RDX,32
        MOV     ECX,IA32_GS_BASE
        WRMSR

#define IA32_FS_BASE    0xC0000100
#define IA32_GS_BASE    0xC0000101

7)      MOV     RAX,FS:[0x1234]
This is RIP relative and completely worthless!
